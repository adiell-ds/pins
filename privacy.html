Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The Parenting Lab Pin Scheduler ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of The Parenting Lab to create and schedule Pins on its own Pinterest account.

What data the app uses

  • The Pinterest access token of the owner's own account, used only to create Pins and read the owner's boards.
  • Pin content created by the owner (images, titles, descriptions, links).

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data about other Pinterest users.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use Pinterest data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

The access token is stored privately on the owner's secured server and is never published. The owner can revoke access at any time in Pinterest settings.
