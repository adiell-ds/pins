Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

ADS Parcerias ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to send and read partnership emails on the owner's own Gmail account.

What data the app uses

  • The Google OAuth token of the owner's own Gmail account, used only to send emails approved by the owner and to read replies to them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access any Google account other than the owner's.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The token is stored privately on the owner's secured server and is never published. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
